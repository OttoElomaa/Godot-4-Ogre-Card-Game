# Enemy AI
timeoutEnemyStartCombat() - modify to create new AI personalities. An AI personality contains the order
in with the AI will perform certain actions in battle or check if it's possible or necessary to perform them.

To create an AI personality, arrange these functions in any way you like. 

await enemySummonCreatures()
await enemyPlayRituals()
await decide_blockers() - defines the algorythm which has the enemy put cards out as blockers.
await playCasters()
await playAttackers()

Feel free to add more functions is necessary.

handleEnemyAttackPlayer(attackCard:Card) - defines the algorythm which has the enemy choose whether to attack the
opponent's cards and which cards to prioritize.

# Card Actions

handlePlayerAttackCreature(target:Card) - called when a card is made attack another card. Modify to change
how the game checks whether a card is targetable by attacks.
resolveAttack(attackCard:Card, targetCard:Card) - called when handlePlayerAttackCreature determines that the 
	targeted card is eligible for being attacked by attackCard. Runs the necessary functions of attackCard and
	targetCard to animate and process combat.
